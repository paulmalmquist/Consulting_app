PROMPT TECHNIQUES




            So what is a prompt?


              01    A prompt is an instruction to the AI.
                    System prompt: every time, like a standing order.
                    User prompt: what we’re doing today.



              02    AI is literal. Tries hard. Wants to please.


              03    Answers may vary when you ask again. AI is “non-deterministic.”


              04    Prompts later in a chat know what you said earlier.


              05    Great prompting helps on any platform, but the platform matters.

PROMPT TECHNIQUES




            Top 5 Essential Prompting Tips

              01    🤓 Treat your AI like a smart, fast, occasionally overeager human
                    intern.


              02    📗 Give context and be speciﬁc - both in what you ask and how
                    you want it to respond.


              03    🔗 Give the AI what you want it to review. If you have a link or doc,
                    give it to the AI.


              04    ↪ Iterate & converse - keep asking, one step at a time.



              05    🆕 Start fresh when needed. New topic, new chat.

Ethics things to know
✅ Duty of Conﬁdentiality
 -   Check your providers’ security & terms
 -   If they meet bar, OK to input conﬁdential info
 -   Public, consumer ChatGPT does NOT meet this bar, unless
     you turn off history (see Appendix)

✅ Duty of Competence & to supervise non-lawyers
- Check the intern’s work! Even the citations

✅ Duty of Candor
- No fake cases
- (debated) duty to disclose to clients                        Calbar guidance


✅ Duty of Diligence
- You can’t unduly delay legal work
- So you must use AI? (I predict law will go there)
