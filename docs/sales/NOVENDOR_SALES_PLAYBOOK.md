## Who We Are Selling To

Novendor targets operators who run complex financial workflows on spreadsheets and duct-taped reporting tools, and who know they need to change but haven't found a vendor who actually understands their world.

Primary verticals:
- **REPE / Fund Managers** — firms managing $500M–$10B AUM, typically 5–50 person teams, drowning in fund admin reporting, LP comms, and portfolio tracking across multiple systems
- **PDS / Construction** — project development and construction teams at mid-market REPE firms and developers; cost tracking, draw management, variance reporting still in Excel
- **Outsourced Fund Admin / Accounting** — firms like BPM, EisnerAmper, IQ-EQ that run fund reporting for multiple REPE clients and are losing margin to manual work

Secondary targets:
- **Legal ops** — firms with real estate transaction workflows (due diligence, closing docs, title review) managed manually
- **Banks and credit** — institutions standing up new AI-augmented data infrastructure (e.g. Live Oak)

---

## The Core Problem We Solve

REPE firms, fund admins, and developers all share a version of the same problem:

> Their financial intelligence is locked in spreadsheets and fragmented systems. Reporting takes days. Data isn't trusted. Leadership makes decisions from stale numbers. Nobody has time to fix it because they're busy maintaining the broken system.

Winston doesn't just visualize data. It replaces the manual step entirely — ingesting raw files, running the numbers, surfacing the answer, and letting the analyst spend their time on decisions instead of formatting.

The specific pain points by vertical are below.

## Vertical 1 — REPE / Fund Managers

### Ideal Customer Profile

- $500M–$5B AUM fund manager or REPE operator
- 5–30 person team (no dedicated data engineering)
- Running investor reporting in Excel + Yardi or MRI
- LP communications built manually each quarter
- CFO, CIO, or VP Finance owns the pain

### The Pain (specific, not generic)

- Quarterly investor reports take 2–3 weeks of analyst time to assemble
- Fund-level IRR and TVPI can't be confirmed quickly because the data lives in multiple places
- LP portal is a static PDF or a Juniper Square view that doesn't match internal numbers
- When an LP asks a one-off question, the answer takes a day to pull
- Asset-level NOI and gross-to-net roll-ups are manual reconciliations every month

### What Winston Replaces

- The "close the books and then build the deck" cycle
- The Excel model that three analysts edit and nobody trusts
- The manual data pull before every LP call
- The Yardi export → paste → format → verify loop

### The ROI Angle

- 10–20 hours per analyst per quarter recovered on reporting
- LP questions answered in minutes instead of days
- One version of the truth across fund, asset, and LP views
- Compliance-grade audit trail on every number

### Objections and Responses

**"We already have Yardi / Juniper Square / Argus."**  
Those are transaction systems. Winston sits on top — it reads what they produce and turns it into decisions. You don't replace your source systems.

**"We're too small / don't have budget."**  
The ROI conversation starts with time. If your CFO or a senior analyst is spending 15 hours a month on report assembly, that's worth more than the engagement cost.

**"We've tried vendor solutions before and they didn't fit."**  
That's the right instinct. Winston is built to be shaped to your workflow — it's not a product you configure, it's a system we implement around your actual data model.

**"We're evaluating Dealpath / Cobalt / etc."**  
Dealpath is deal pipeline. Cobalt is read-only dashboards. Winston is a write layer — it executes and tracks, not just reports.

### Demo Angles

1. Live fund dashboard with IRR, TVPI, DPI pulling from a real data file — show the number populating in real time
2. LP Q&A mode — ask Winston "what's the net return on Fund II for the LPs who invested in 2019?" and show the answer
3. Report generation — trigger a quarterly investor report and show the PDF build in under 60 seconds

### Outreach Angles (LinkedIn / Email)

- CMBS delinquency signal → "we're seeing stress in office exposure across mid-market funds — how are your LPs asking about it?"
- Fed rate environment → "higher for longer is squeezing levered IRRs — are you running sensitivities, or waiting to see it in actuals?"
- Talent pressure → "your best analyst is spending 40% of their time on report assembly — that's a retention risk"

## Vertical 2 — PDS / Construction

### Ideal Customer Profile

- Development or construction management team at a REPE firm or mid-market developer
- Managing 5–50 active projects
- Cost tracking in Procore, Sage, or Excel
- Draw requests and variance reports built manually
- VP of Development, Director of Construction, or CFO owns the pain

### The Pain (specific, not generic)

- Monthly draw requests take 3–5 days to assemble and review
- Budget-vs-actual reports are built in Excel with multiple manual data pulls from Procore/Sage
- When a project goes over budget, nobody knows until the draw comes in
- Owner's rep reporting to lenders and LPs is a separate manual exercise each month
- Change order tracking is inconsistent across projects

### What Winston Replaces

- The monthly draw assembly process
- The budget variance spreadsheet
- The owner's rep report template someone fills in manually
- The "export from Procore, paste, format, send" sequence

### The ROI Angle

- Draw approval cycle compressed from 5 days to same-day for clean draws
- Budget overrun caught at line-item level before it becomes a draw dispute
- Owner's rep reports generated automatically with audit-ready data

### Objections and Responses

**"We use Procore for everything."**  
Procore captures the data. Winston makes it usable for owners, lenders, and LPs — the people Procore wasn't built for.

**"We're mid-build and can't change systems."**  
Winston doesn't change your systems. It reads what you already have and adds the intelligence layer on top. You can be live in days.

### Demo Angle

Live project cost dashboard — show a real Excel upload converting to a variance report with budget alerts flagged at the line-item level.

## Messaging Rules

1. **Lead with their world, not our product.** Open with a specific observation about their business, market stress, or workflow pain — not a product pitch.
2. **One point per message.** Don't list features. Make one sharp claim and prove it.
3. **Ask a question that requires an honest answer.** "Are your quarterly reports taking more than two weeks to close?" is harder to ignore than "I'd love to connect."
4. **Keep it under 80 words on LinkedIn.** Longer messages get skimmed or ignored.
5. **Follow up exactly once, four business days later.** No third message unless they respond.
6. **Reference a specific market signal when possible.** CMBS stress, cap rate expansion, rate environment, talent pressure.

## What a Winning Engagement Looks Like

1. First call: 30 minutes. Understand their current reporting workflow. Identify the biggest manual bottleneck.
2. Second call: 45 minutes demo. Show Winston against their actual data model if they'll share a sample.
3. Proposal: Fixed-scope assessment (2–4 weeks) that maps their current state and delivers a build plan. Price: $15–30K.
4. Implementation: Build phase, 4–12 weeks depending on scope. Price: $40–150K.
5. Ongoing: Monthly retainer or per-seat license for Winston access + support.
