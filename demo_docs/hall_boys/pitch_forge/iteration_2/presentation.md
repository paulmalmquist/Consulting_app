# Claude Inside Hallboys
*Five workflows, one scorecard, 60 days — measured against jobs you ran last quarter*

## Slide 1: Claude Inside Hallboys
Five workflows, one scorecard, 60 days — measured against jobs you ran last quarter

## Slide 2: What we are not proposing
No new ERP. No headcount cuts. No black-box AP automation. No custom build. No data leaving your Claude workspace.

## Slide 3: The Hallboys reality we are designing around
**What you have**
- Acumatica as system of record — stays the source of truth
- 3-person IT team — no spare capacity for a 6-month rollout
- One AP clerk processing every invoice that comes in
- Claude already in use across estimating, ops, finance
- No shared skill library, no audit trail on Claude prompts today

**What we will not touch**
- No replacement of Acumatica or any existing system
- No new headcount required
- No removal of human approval on AP, contracts, or schedule
- No data leaves the Hallboys Claude workspace
- No long onboarding — pilots fit inside existing weekly meetings

## Slide 4: Five workflows. Same team. Same systems.
Each one ties to cost, capacity, or risk — and each one is measured against a real Hallboys baseline in week one.

## Slide 5: Workflow 1 — Subcontractor quote comparison
**3.25 hrs** Estimator time saved per multi-vendor bid package (estimate, to be validated week 1)
Estimator drops 3-5 sub quotes into Claude. Pilot kill: <1.5 hrs saved per package by day 30.

## Slide 6: Workflow 2 — Equipment scheduling coordination
**$5,400** Cost of one idle crew day on a typical Hallboys mid-size job (estimate, to be confirmed)
Baseline: pull last 90 days of schedule changes from Acumatica, re-run them.

## Slide 7: Workflow 3 — AP exception triage (assisted, not automated)
**0 writes** Claude never posts to Acumatica — the AP clerk owns every approval
Target: same clerk reviews 1.6x current invoice volume per day with no drop in catch rate. Shadow mode for 30 days first.

## Slide 8: Workflow 4 — Project closeout document checks
**1 retainage delay** Avoiding one retainage delay per quarter is the breakeven for this workflow alone
Pilot kill: <3 real missing items surfaced across the first 5 closeouts.

## Slide 9: Workflow 5 — Claude skill governance
**12-15 hrs/wk** Estimated rework today across Hallboys Claude users (5 users × ~3 hrs/week, to be validated)
Owner: IT lead. Cadence: monthly skill review. Escalation: AP/finance changes need clerk + controller sign-off.

## Slide 10: What happens when Claude is wrong
**Where the human still owns the call**
- Quote comparison: estimator picks the winner
- Schedule conflicts: scheduler approves every change
- AP: clerk posts to Acumatica — Claude never writes
- Closeout: PM signs off the package
- Skills: IT lead approves every skill version

**What gets logged and reviewed**
- Every Claude session in shared workspace is auditable
- Misses captured in 1-page weekly review (15 min)
- Repeated misses flow back into the skill as a fix
- If a skill drifts >2 misses/week, pulled until repaired
- Quarterly review: kill any skill not earning its keep

## Slide 11: What changes Monday morning — and how we measure it
| Workflow | Owner | Baseline source | Kill threshold by day 30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote comparison | Lead estimator | Last quarter's bid log (re-run) | <1.5 hrs saved per package |
| Equipment scheduling | Scheduler | Last 90 days schedule changes in Acumatica | <1 caught conflict/month |
| AP triage | AP clerk | 30 days historical invoices, shadow mode | <1.3x volume at same catch rate |
| Closeout checks | PM on closing job | First 5 closeouts in pilot | <3 real missing items surfaced |

## Slide 12: Two workflows. 60 days. One scorecard.
Quote comparison + closeout checks. Real Hallboys baselines. Stop at any point. Decide from data.
