# Claude Inside Hallboys
*Five workflows where Claude saves hours this quarter — without changing Acumatica*

## Slide 1: Claude Inside Hallboys
Five workflows where Claude saves hours this quarter — without changing Acumatica

## Slide 2: What we are not proposing
No new ERP. No headcount cuts. No black-box AP automation. No custom build.

## Slide 3: Hallboys reality we are designing around
**What you have**
- Acumatica as system of record — stays the source of truth
- 3-person IT team, no spare capacity for a 6-month rollout
- One AP clerk processing every invoice that comes in
- Claude already in use — but no shared rules or skill library
- Field, office, and finance work happens on different cadences

**What we will not touch**
- No replacement of Acumatica or any existing system
- No new headcount required
- No removal of human approval on AP, contracts, or schedule
- No long onboarding — pilots run inside existing weekly meetings
- No data leaves Hallboys' Claude workspace

## Slide 4: Five workflows. Same team. Same systems.
Each one targets cost, capacity, or risk — measured against today's baseline.

## Slide 5: Workflow 1 — Subcontractor quote comparison
**4 hrs → 35 min** Per multi-vendor bid package, estimator time (medium confidence)
Estimator drops 3-5 sub quotes into Claude. Output: side-by-side scope, line-item deltas, missing items, risk flags. Estimator still picks the winner.

## Slide 6: Workflow 2 — Equipment scheduling coordination
**2-3 saves/wk** Avoided crew idle days from caught conflicts (medium confidence)
Weekly schedule and equipment list go into Claude. Output: vendor overlaps, missing deliveries, dependency breaks, dates that don't match the critical path.

## Slide 7: Workflow 3 — AP exception triage (assisted, not automated)
**1 clerk, 2x volume** Same AP clerk handles more invoices without losing review quality (medium confidence)
Every invoice still flows through Acumatica. Claude sits beside the clerk: extracts vendor, amount, PO match, scope match, flags duplicates and pricing anomalies.

## Slide 8: Workflow 4 — Project closeout document checks
**<2 hrs** To verify a closeout package vs. the required checklist (high confidence)
PM uploads the closeout folder. Claude returns: missing items, inconsistent dates, lien waivers without matching pay apps, warranty letters absent.

## Slide 9: Workflow 5 — Claude skill governance
**1 shared library** Replacing scattered prompts across the team (high confidence)
Today every Claude user writes their own prompts. Proposal: a small set of versioned skills (one per workflow above), owned by IT, used by everyone.

## Slide 10: What changes Monday morning
| Workflow | Owner | Where it runs | First measurable signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote comparison | Lead estimator | Claude desktop, beside Acumatica | Bid cycle time per package |
| Equipment scheduling | Scheduler | Weekly schedule review meeting | Conflicts caught before Monday |
| AP exception triage | AP clerk | Beside Acumatica AP screen | Invoices per clerk per day |
| Closeout checks | PM on closing job | At handover, before final pay app | Closeout cycle from punch to final |
| Skill governance | IT (3-person team) | Shared Claude workspace | Number of prompts replaced by skills |

## Slide 11: Pilot plan — 60 days, no platform commitment
**Days 1-30**
- Pick 2 of the 5 workflows (recommend: quote comparison + closeout checks)
- Run beside the human owner — no system writes
- Capture baseline: hours, errors caught, items missed
- IT publishes v1 skills into shared workspace
- Weekly 30-min review with one estimator and one PM

**Days 31-60**
- Add a third workflow if first two clear baseline
- Sarat sees a one-page scorecard: hours saved, errors caught, prompts retired
- Decision point: continue, adjust scope, or stop — no lock-in
- Skill library handed to IT for ongoing ownership
- Not available: training cost projection until pilot baseline lands

## Slide 12: Same team. Same systems. More throughput.
Two workflows, 60 days, one scorecard. Decide from data, not a slide.
